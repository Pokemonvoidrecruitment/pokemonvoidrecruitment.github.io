Privacy

This website is a preview. Live recruitment data handling is still being prepared and will be reviewed before applications open.

Your draft in this preview

The form keeps your answers in this browser tab using session storage. The website does not send those answers to the recruitment team while submissions are disabled.

Refreshing or moving between pages in the same tab keeps your draft. Use Clear draft on the application page to remove it. Closing the tab normally clears it, although a browser may restore it when reopening a previous session.

Drafts are not linked to a signed-in account in this preview. Anyone using the same open tab can view them.

When applications open

The application will ask for a Discord account, preferred name, timezone, broad age bracket, experience, availability and answers about your selected roles. Pronouns, personal projects and several work-example fields are optional.

The intended reviewers are authorised recruitment Directors. Application answers and interview conversations will not be placed in the public website repository.

The live privacy notice still needs to explain retention, deletion requests and the contact route for privacy questions before submissions are enabled.

Interview conversations

Interview messaging is disabled. The team is still deciding how access to those conversations will be protected. The preview does not implement or promise an encryption system.

Work links and hosting

This form accepts links instead of file uploads. Links you open are handled by their own websites and privacy policies.

The site contains no analytics or advertising scripts. The hosting provider may still process ordinary connection information when serving the pages.
