About this preview

The recruitment website is still being prepared. You can try the form, choose roles and review your answers. Sign-in, application submission and interview messages are not available yet.

The form saves a draft in this browser tab. Refreshing the page keeps it; use Clear draft to remove it. This is not a submitted application, and the team cannot see it.

The interview and Director pages include clearly labelled fictional examples so the team can review the layouts. They contain no real recruitment records.

Applying to the team

Pokemon Void is a PokedexFillers fan project. All roles are voluntary and unpaid. Applicants of any age are welcome.

Programmer, Move Animator, Spriter and Music are the open roles. Choose as many as you want in one application; the form asks questions for each selected role.

  1. Tell us your preferred name, timezone and age bracket.
  2. Choose your roles.
  3. Tell us about your experience, interest in the project, approach to feedback and availability.
  4. Answer the questions for your roles.
  5. Review your answers. You can go back and edit any section.

There is no timer. Give yourself enough time to answer in your own words.

Work examples

Share links to work that shows what you can do. For art and music, you can describe your experience if you do not have a portfolio.

Check that the recruitment team can open your links. You do not need to upload large videos here; those can be arranged separately if needed.

After applying

When submissions open, the Directors will review applications through this portal. You will be able to check progress under My application using the Discord account you applied with.

If the team wants to continue, you will move to a private interview ticket for follow-up questions. Some roles also need a live conversation on Discord. Successful applicants receive personal onboarding with the team.

These features are being prepared. Finishing the current preview does not put you in the review queue.

Discord sign-in

Once available, sign-in will take you to Discord to connect your account. The recruitment form will never ask for your Discord password or authentication codes.

If you are returning to an application, use the same Discord account you originally applied with.
